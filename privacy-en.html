Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

권용우 (the "Operator") values the personal information of users of "Cerebrew" (the "App") and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws.

1. Using the App without signing in (guest)

  • Your records, settings and favorites are stored only on your device and are not sent to the Operator.
  • They are removed from your device when you delete the App or tap Settings ▸ Clear all records.

2. Information collected when you create an account

  • Email address: sign-in, account verification, replies to inquiries
  • Password: sign-in (stored only as a one-way hash that even the Operator cannot read)
  • Play records (puzzle ID, correct or not, time taken, hints used, date): sync across devices, records and statistics, improving puzzles
  • Weekly ranking records (a nickname made from part of your email, time taken): leaderboard
  • Subscription plan and expiry: providing features
  • Sign-in times: preventing abuse, understanding usage
  • Retention: when you delete your account, all of the above is deleted immediately. You can delete it anytime in Settings ▸ Account ▸ Delete account.

3. Information received from all users (not personally identifiable)

  • Problem reports: only when you send one — puzzle ID, reason, note and app language (plus account ID if signed in)
  • Ad result counts: whether ads were shown or failed, counted only by date, device type and ad type. We do not store who saw them or any device identifier.

4. Advertising (free plan)

  • The App shows Google AdMob ads. To provide ads, Google may process your advertising identifier (if you allow it), approximate location estimated from your IP address, and ad interaction data.
  • If you decline "Allow tracking" on iPhone, you will only see non-personalized ads. In regions where consent is required, you can change it anytime in Settings ▸ Ad privacy settings.
  • How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Subscription payments

Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). The Operator does not receive card numbers or other payment details.

6. Sharing and processors

  • The Operator does not sell or provide your personal information to third parties.
  • Processors: Amazon Web Services (servers and data storage), Google (ads), Apple and Google (payments)

7. International transfer

  • Recipient: Amazon Web Services, Inc. (https://aws.amazon.com/contact-us/)
  • Country: Japan (AWS Asia Pacific Tokyo region)
  • When and how: each time you create an account or sync records, sent over the internet (HTTPS encrypted)
  • Items: those listed in section 2
  • Purpose: server operation and data storage
  • Retention: until you delete your account
  • How to refuse: if you use the App as a guest without an account, nothing is transferred abroad.

8. Your rights

You may access, correct or delete your personal information, or ask us to stop processing it, at any time. You can delete your account directly in the App; send other requests to the email below.

9. Security measures

One-way password hashing, HTTPS encryption in transit, restricted server access, and data backups.

10. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. Users under 14 should not create an account and should use the App as a guest.

11. Privacy officer and contact

  • Operator (privacy officer): 권용우
  • Email: sawooggang@naver.com

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will announce it in the App at least 7 days before it takes effect.
